1 Introduction & Core Philosophy

AccessCalc ("the Application") is an advanced, fully accessible multi-mode calculator designed for Microsoft Windows. Engineered from the ground up by independent developer Bibek Sharma in Nepal, the application provides blind, low-vision, and all users with a robust, accessible mathematical computation platform.

Privacy is a fundamental human right. In AccessCalc, privacy is not an afterthought or an optional toggle—it is built into every architectural decision. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is processed, where it resides, and your complete rights and controls as a user.

Key Principle: AccessCalc operates on a strict privacy-by-design model. You do not need to create an account, log in, or disclose any personal identity to utilize the full capabilities of AccessCalc.

2 Information We Do Not Collect

To ensure absolute clarity, AccessCalc does not collect, harvest, monitor, or transmit any of the following:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, phone numbers, home addresses, or social media accounts during ordinary usage.
  • No User Account or Authentication Credentials: AccessCalc requires no registration, username, password, or cloud profile.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Analytics: We do not log which modes you open, which buttons you press, how long you use the app, or your usage habits.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not collect MAC addresses, device serial numbers, BIOS identifiers, or unique advertising IDs.
  • No Geographic Location: We do not request, access, or track your GPS coordinates, IP-based physical location, or WiFi positioning.
  • No Mathematical Profiling: Your calculations, numbers, currency conversions, formulas, and graphing coordinates remain solely your private property and never leave your device.

3 Local Data Storage & Calculation History

AccessCalc is a locally executing Windows client application. Any persistent data generated during use is stored exclusively on your local computer:

A. Application Configuration & User Preferences

To preserve your preferred settings between sessions, AccessCalc writes a small local JSON configuration file (config.json). This configuration file stores:

  • The last active calculator mode (e.g., Standard, Scientific, Programmer, Matrix).
  • Your preferred speech echo state (enabled or disabled).
  • Your chosen speech rate and voice volume preferences.

This file is stored in your private Windows user profile under:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\BlindTechNexus.AccessCalc\LocalState\config.json

This data is never synchronized with external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

B. Calculation History

AccessCalc maintains a calculation history during active sessions so you can review recent computations across all calculator modes (Standard, Scientific, Programmer, Matrix, Equations, etc.).

  • Session Retention: Up to 100 recent calculation items are kept in local memory.
  • User Control: You can open the Calculation History flyout (Ctrl+H) and click "Clear All History" at any time to permanently wipe stored calculations from memory.

4 Audio Speech & Accessibility Services

AccessCalc provides comprehensive accessibility features, including full compatibility with assistive screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, Windows Narrator) and an integrated Audio Speech Feedback Echo.

  • Offline Speech Synthesis: Spoken audio feedback is generated locally on your computer via the standard Windows Speech Synthesis engine (System.Speech / SAPI). No internet connection is used or required to generate spoken output.
  • No Audio Recording or Microphones: AccessCalc never accesses or activates your microphone, never records ambient audio, and never captures your voice.
  • Screen Reader Live Regions: Keyboard announcements and live mathematical readouts are routed directly through Windows UI Automation (UIA) APIs to your active screen reader on-device.

5 Network Usage & External Services

AccessCalc requires network access exclusively for two specific, optional functions:

A. Live Currency Exchange Rates

In the Currency Converter mode, AccessCalc connects via secure HTTPS to a public financial exchange rate API to download current baseline conversion rates for world currencies.

  • Data Transmitted: Anonymous read-only HTTP GET requests for exchange rate tables.
  • No User Identification: No personal details, account data, or conversion amounts are transmitted.
  • Offline Resilience: If an internet connection is unavailable, AccessCalc uses built-in baseline rates and alerts the user, ensuring the app remains fully functional offline.

B. Send Feedback Dialog (User-Initiated)

AccessCalc includes a voluntary "Send Feedback" dialog on the application toolbar allowing users to report bugs, suggest features, or submit feedback directly to the developer.

  • Voluntary Submission: Feedback is submitted only when you explicitly click the "Send Feedback" button.
  • Submitted Data: The transmission contains solely what you enter in the form:
    • Your provided Name (required).
    • Your optional Contact Email address (if you wish to receive a reply).
    • Your typed Feedback message (required).
    • An automated timestamp and application name identifier.
  • Transmission Method: Form data is securely transmitted over TLS-encrypted HTTPS using the Telegram Bot API directly to the developer's administrative inbox.
  • Purpose of Use: This information is utilized strictly to understand user feedback, diagnose bugs, improve AccessCalc, and reply to you if an email was provided.

7 Third-Party Services & Advertising

AccessCalc is an independent, ad-free utility:

  • No Advertising Networks: The application contains zero advertisements, banners, pop-ups, or affiliate trackers.
  • No Third-Party SDK Trackers: We do not integrate Facebook SDK, Google Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or any commercial profiling SDKs.
  • No Data Sales or Commercial Transfers: We do not sell, rent, license, or barter your data to third parties under any circumstances.

8 Data Security & Retention

We implement rigorous technical and operational standards to ensure the integrity of your data:

  • Transport Layer Security: All external network calls (currency exchange queries and feedback submissions) utilize modern TLS encryption (HTTPS) to safeguard data in transit against interception.
  • Local System Isolation: Configuration files are stored within the secure, sandboxed Windows application data directory isolated to your Windows user account.
  • Retention Period: In-memory calculation history is ephemeral and automatically discarded when you exit the application or invoke the clear command. Feedback messages received by the developer are retained only as long as necessary to address the suggestion or fix the reported defect, after which they are securely archived or purged.

9 User Rights, Choices & Control

You have full autonomy over your data while utilizing AccessCalc:

  • Clearing Calculation History: Open the Calculation History flyout (Ctrl+H) and activate the Clear All History button.
  • Resetting Application Settings: You can reset preferences at any time by deleting the local config.json file located in your application data directory. AccessCalc will automatically regenerate default preferences upon the next launch.
  • Disabling Audio Echo: Toggle audible voice speech feedback off or on anytime using the toolbar toggle or shortcut Ctrl+S.
  • Anonymous Feedback: When submitting feedback, you are not required to provide a real email address or real name. Feedback can be submitted with pseudonymous details.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If you previously submitted feedback containing personal contact details and wish to have that record deleted, contact the developer through the in-app feedback channel or via email, and the record will be erased within 30 days.

10 Children's Privacy

AccessCalc is a general-audience educational and computational tool suitable for students, educators, professionals, and users of all ages. The application does not intentionally solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by local jurisdiction, including GDPR guidelines). If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has submitted feedback containing personal data, they may contact the developer for prompt deletion.
